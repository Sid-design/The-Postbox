# Privacy Policy - The Postbox

**Effective Date:** January 15, 2025

## Introduction

The Postbox ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

## Information We Collect

### Personal Information
- **Google Account Information**: When you sign in with Google, we collect your email address and name
- **Device Information**: Device type, operating system, and unique device identifiers
- **Usage Data**: How you interact with the app, newsletters you read, and preferences

### Automatically Collected Information
- **App Usage Analytics**: Firebase Analytics collects anonymous usage patterns
- **Crash Reports**: Automatic error reporting to improve app stability
- **Newsletter Content**: We store newsletter content you subscribe to (with your permission)

## How We Use Your Information

### Core Functionality
- **Authentication**: To verify your identity and provide secure access
- **Newsletter Delivery**: To fetch and display newsletters from your Gmail
- **Personalization**: To customize your reading experience

### Analytics & Improvement
- **App Performance**: Monitor crashes and performance issues
- **User Experience**: Understand how users interact with features
- **Feature Development**: Guide future improvements

### Communication
- **Push Notifications**: Send alerts about new newsletters (with your consent)
- **Support**: Respond to your inquiries and provide customer service

## Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your personal information to third parties, except in the following circumstances:

### Service Providers
- **Firebase**: For analytics, crash reporting, and push notifications
- **Google APIs**: To access your Gmail for newsletter content
- **App Store**: Apple may collect anonymized usage data

### Legal Requirements
- If required by law or to protect our rights
- In case of merger, acquisition, or sale of assets

## Data Security

We implement appropriate security measures to protect your personal information:

- **Encryption**: All data transmission uses HTTPS/TLS
- **Secure Storage**: Sensitive data stored using platform security features
- **Access Controls**: Limited access to personal data on a need-to-know basis
- **Regular Updates**: Security measures are regularly reviewed and updated

## Data Retention

- **Personal Information**: Retained as long as your account is active
- **Usage Analytics**: Anonymized and retained for up to 26 months
- **Newsletter Content**: Stored until you delete it or close your account

## Your Rights

You have the right to:
- **Access**: Request a copy of your personal data
- **Correction**: Update inaccurate information
- **Deletion**: Request deletion of your data
- **Portability**: Export your data in a usable format
- **Opt-out**: Disable analytics or push notifications

## Children's Privacy

Our service is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Effective Date".

## Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at:
- Email: support@thepostbox.app
- App Support: Settings → Contact Support
