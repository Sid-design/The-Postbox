Privacy Policy

The Postbox — your newsletter companion

Effective date: June 1, 2026

The Postbox ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what information the app collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. By using The Postbox you agree to the practices described here.

1. Information We Collect

Information you provide via Google Sign-In

  • Account details: your email address and name, obtained when you sign in with Google.
  • Gmail access: with your consent, read-only access to your Gmail to identify and fetch newsletters from senders you approve. We do not read, store, or process non-newsletter mail beyond what is needed to detect newsletter senders.

Information created by using the app

  • Newsletter content & senders: newsletters from senders you subscribe to, and your list of approved/blocked senders, stored so the app can display your inbox.
  • Preferences: settings such as theme and notification choices.
  • Push token: an Expo push token so we can deliver notifications you opt into.

Diagnostics

  • Crash & error reports: if the app encounters an error, technical diagnostic data (e.g. error type, device model, OS version) is sent to Sentry to help us fix problems. These reports are not used to advertise to you.
We do not use Firebase, advertising SDKs, or third-party behavioral analytics, and we do not sell your personal information.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Authentication — to verify your identity and keep you signed in securely.
  • Core functionality — to fetch, organize, and display newsletters from your approved senders.
  • Notifications — to send push alerts about new newsletters, only if you enable them.
  • Reliability — to diagnose crashes and improve stability.
  • Support — to respond to your questions.

3. How Your Information Is Stored

Account data, your sender list, and newsletter content are stored on our backend (hosted on Fly.io) in a PostgreSQL database. Authentication tokens are kept in your device's secure storage. All network traffic uses HTTPS/TLS.

4. Service Providers We Share With

We share data only as needed to operate the service:

  • Google — Gmail API access to retrieve your newsletters (privacy policy).
  • Fly.io — backend and database hosting.
  • Sentry — crash and error diagnostics (privacy policy).
  • Expo — delivery of push notifications via the Expo Push Service (privacy policy).
  • Apple — Apple Push Notification service for iOS delivery (privacy policy).

We may also disclose information if required by law, or to protect the rights, safety, or property of our users or us.

5. Google API Limited Use

The Postbox's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Gmail data is used solely to provide the in-app newsletter reading experience and is never sold or used for advertising.

6. Data Retention & Deletion

  • Your account data and newsletter content are retained while your account is active.
  • You can disconnect your account at any time from Settings, which removes your stored data from our backend.
  • You may also request deletion by contacting us at the email below.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your region, you may have the right to access, correct, export, or delete your personal data, and to opt out of notifications. To exercise these rights, contact us.

8. Children's Privacy

The Postbox is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected here with a new effective date.
